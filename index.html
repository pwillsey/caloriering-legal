Privacy Policy

CalorieRing does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All food logs and nutrition targets are stored locally on your device.

The camera is used only for scanning food barcodes. No images are stored or transmitted.

Food searches and barcode lookups query the Open Food Facts public database. Only the search term or barcode is sent — no personal information is included in these requests.

There are no user accounts, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs.

If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Questions? Reach out at support@caloriering.app.
